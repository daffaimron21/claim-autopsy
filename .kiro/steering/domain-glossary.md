# Domain glossary (all data in this repo is synthetic)

- **Claim (klaim)**: a hospital's request for payment to BPJS for one patient stay.
- **Returned claim**: a claim BPJS sent back with status *pending* or *dispute* and a short reason.
- **ICD-10**: diagnosis codes. **ICD-9-CM**: procedure codes.
- **INA-CBG**: the case-based group code that decides the payment tariff.
- **Coder (koder)**: hospital staff who assign codes and fix returned claims.
- **Autopsy report**: our output. Primary cause, secondary causes, evidence, fix, resubmit verdict.
